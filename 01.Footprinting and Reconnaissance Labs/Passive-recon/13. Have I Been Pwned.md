# Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) - Breach Data Research

## Objective
To identify whether a target email address has been exposed in publicly known data breaches using Have I Been Pwned (HIBP).

## Tool Used
- Have I Been Pwned (HIBP)

## Methodology

### Step 1: Access HIBP
Navigate to the Have I Been Pwned website and enter the target email address.

### Step 2: Search for Breaches
Click the **Check** button to analyze whether the email address appears in known data breaches.

### Step 3: Review Results
Analyze the breach history, affected organizations, and exposed data types.

## Findings

- The target email address was found in multiple publicly disclosed data breaches.
- Total Breaches Detected: **95**
- The breach timeline shows historical incidents where the email address was exposed.
- One of the identified breaches involved **DentaQuest**.
- Exposed information may include:
  - Email Addresses
  - Names
  - Phone Numbers
  - Addresses
  - Other personal information depending on the breach

---

## Security Impact

Data breaches can lead to:

- Credential Stuffing Attacks
- Account Takeovers
- Phishing Campaigns
- Identity Theft
- Social Engineering Attacks

---

## Recommendations

- Change passwords for affected accounts.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Use unique passwords for each service.
- Monitor accounts for suspicious activity.
- Regularly check breach exposure status.

---

## Conclusion

Have I Been Pwned is a valuable passive reconnaissance resource for identifying exposed email addresses in publicly known breaches. The analysis revealed that the tested email address appeared in **95 data breaches**, highlighting the importance of strong password hygiene and continuous security monitoring.
