# 🔍 Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) – Breach Data Research

## 🎯 Objective

To determine whether a target email address has been exposed in publicly disclosed data breaches using the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) platform.

---

## 🛠️ Tool Used

* Have I Been Pwned (HIBP)

---

## 📚 Overview

Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) is a widely recognized breach-notification service that aggregates data from publicly disclosed security incidents and data breaches. It enables security professionals to identify whether email addresses have been exposed and assess potential risks associated with compromised accounts.

---

## 🔬 Methodology

### 📌 Step 1: Access HIBP

Navigate to the HIBP website and enter the target email address into the search field.

### 📌 Step 2: Perform Breach Search

Click the **Check** button to determine whether the email address appears in any known data breaches.

### 📌 Step 3: Analyze Results

Review the breach history, affected organizations, breach dates, and categories of exposed information.

## ⚠️ Security Impact Assessment

Compromised email addresses can increase the likelihood of:

### 🎯 Credential Stuffing Attacks

Attackers may attempt previously leaked credentials on other services.

### 🔓 Account Takeovers

Reused passwords can lead to unauthorized account access.

### 🎣 Phishing Campaigns

Attackers can craft targeted phishing emails using exposed information.

### 🕵️ Identity Theft

Personal information may be leveraged for fraudulent activities.

### 🤝 Social Engineering Attacks

Exposed data can improve the success rate of manipulation-based attacks.

---

## 🛡️ Security Recommendations

To reduce risk associated with breach exposure:

* ✅ Change passwords for affected accounts immediately.
* ✅ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
* ✅ Use unique passwords for every online service.
* ✅ Monitor accounts for suspicious activity.
* ✅ Utilize a password manager for credential management.
* ✅ Regularly review breach exposure using trusted monitoring services.
* ✅ Remove unused accounts whenever possible.

---

## 📈 Risk Analysis

The presence of an email address in **95 separate breach datasets** significantly increases the attack surface available to threat actors. Repeated exposure across multiple breaches suggests that strong credential management practices and continuous monitoring are essential to reduce security risks.
