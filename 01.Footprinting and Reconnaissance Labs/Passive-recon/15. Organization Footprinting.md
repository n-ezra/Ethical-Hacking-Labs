# 🏢 Organization Footprinting (VulnWeb Case Study)

## 📌 Overview

Organization Footprinting is a passive reconnaissance technique used to gather detailed information about a target organization's digital footprint, including domains, infrastructure, technologies, and ownership.

This case study demonstrates footprinting using **vulnweb.com**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Identify organization behind the target
* Discover associated assets
* Map infrastructure and technologies
* Understand attack surface

---
