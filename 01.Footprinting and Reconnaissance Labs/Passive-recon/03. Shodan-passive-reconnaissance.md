# Shodan Fundamentals and Passive Reconnaissance

## Overview

Shodan is a search engine designed to discover and index internet-connected devices and services. Unlike traditional search engines that focus on websites, Shodan collects information about publicly accessible servers, routers, webcams, IoT devices, industrial control systems, cloud infrastructure, and various network services.

Cybersecurity professionals use Shodan for passive reconnaissance, attack surface discovery, security assessments, threat intelligence, and asset monitoring.

## What is Passive Reconnaissance?

Passive reconnaissance is the process of collecting information about a target without directly interacting with its systems.

Shodan is considered a passive reconnaissance tool because it uses previously indexed information rather than actively scanning targets during searches.


```text

```

### Microsoft IIS Servers

```text

```

### HTTPS Services


### HTTP Services


### SSH Services


